For parents and teachers

Fun first. Learning built in.

Captain Dad Games are free browser games for primary school kids. Every game is play-tested by real kids, and the learning sits inside the fun, so practice feels like playing.

Our promises

  • No ads

    No banners, no pop-ups, no in-game purchases.

  • No sign-ups

    Kids type a first name or nickname. No emails or passwords.

  • Saved on your device

    Progress stays in the browser on the device they play on.

  • Kind by design

    Mistakes show the right answer. No timers, no pressure.

In the classroom

  • Runs in the web browser on school iPads, Chromebooks and laptops. Nothing to install.
  • Put it on the big screen and let the class shout the answers.
  • Questions can be read aloud, which helps early and reluctant readers.
  • Each device keeps its own riders, so a shared iPad can hold a few kids.

At home

  • Best on an iPad or computer. On a phone, turn it sideways.
  • Add it to the home screen to play full screen like an app.
  • A park takes a few minutes, which is great for a quick practice.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. There are no ads, subscriptions or in-game purchases.

What information do you collect?

No accounts and no personal details. Kids' names and progress are saved in the browser on their own device, and never sent to us. Sum Shredders counts visits anonymously with Vercel Web Analytics, which uses no cookies and nothing that identifies your child. This website doesn't use analytics at all.

Which devices work best?

Tablets and computers. Phones work too when turned sideways.

Can I suggest a game or report a problem?

Please do! Send Captain Dad an email and he'll take a look.
