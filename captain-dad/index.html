Meet Captain Dad

Captain Dad is a real dad who builds games at the kitchen table. His two boys, Wiz and Sonny, are the official game testers. They pick the ideas, try every level and tell him when something is boring.

Sum Shredders started because maths practice needed more bike jumps. Now there's a whole shelf of games on the way.
